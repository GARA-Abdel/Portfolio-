Young Scientist & Aspiring Engineer

Abdel Aziz Adama GARA

A young scientist and aspiring engineer from Burkina Faso, I am passionate about mathematics, physics, artificial intelligence, robotics, and emerging technologies. My scientific curiosity drives me to explore the connections between fundamental sciences, engineering, and technology in order to turn ideas into concrete solutions.

Currently a student at the Lycée Scientifique National de Bobo-Dioulasso, I am pursuing an intensive scientific education in mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, and computer science. In 2026, I completed my Première C (11th grade, science track) ranked first out of 35 students in my class.

My academic path is particularly oriented toward mechanical engineering, mechatronics, autonomous systems, and aerospace engineering. I am also developing a strong interest in artificial intelligence, robotics, and sustainable technologies.

Through my projects, research, and activities, I strive to strengthen my ability to understand problems, design solutions, and experiment with ideas. Among my works are a gas-leak prevention system developed as part of a team, Passeport Carbone, a digital tool for assessing greenhouse gas emissions for small and medium-sized enterprises, a project combining bioenergy and hydroponics, and the design of an institutional website. My journey also includes participation in national and international scientific competitions, notably ISTEC 2026 and IRCYS 2026, as well as international competitions in chemistry and computer science.
