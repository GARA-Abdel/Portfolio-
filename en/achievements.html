    About the competition

    ISTEC is an international scientific competition for young people interested in science, technology, and engineering. Organized by the Bandung Creative Society in Indonesia, it encourages scientific innovation and the design of concrete solutions. The 2026 edition was the seventh edition. The competition is open to participants aged 10 to 24, in teams of up to five members, with a mentor. Projects and presentations are in English.

    ISTEC offers two tracks: "Product, Technology and Engineering" for prototypes, devices, software, and technological systems, and "Research, Science and Theory" for scientific research work. The selection process includes a 250 word abstract phase, then additional evaluations with a presentation video, regional and national selections, before the international final. The final takes the form of a scientific exhibition with a booth, an A1 poster, and demonstration equipment, with five minutes of presentation and five minutes of questions with the jury.

    Our participation

    Our team of five students submitted the project Gas Leak Prevention System, a detection and prevention system for domestic gas leaks. After the dossier selection phase, we were qualified for the ISTEC 2026 Grand Final. Time and resource constraints prevented the team from traveling to Bali. This qualification nonetheless remains an important milestone in our research journey.

    My role

    Team coordination, communication, research, and project presentation.

    About the competition

    IRCYS is an international competition dedicated to scientific research among young people, organized by the Bandung Creative Society. Open to participants aged 10 to 24, individually or in teams of up to five members, with a mentor. Official language: English. Three categories: "Theoretical Studies, Science and Social", "Applied Studies, Engineering and Technology", and "Humanity Studies, Humanity and Social Intervention".

    The selection process has three phases: Initial Screening with a 250 word abstract and plagiarism check, Full Paper Review with a complete scientific article, then the international final. The final evaluation is based on the scientific article for 40 percent and the oral presentation with questions for 60 percent. The presentation lasts five minutes, followed by five minutes of exchanges. Awards include gold, silver, and bronze medals, special distinctions, and prizes.

    Our participation

    Participation in the IRCYS 2026 international final with the Gas Leak Prevention System project developed as a team. This experience represents an international milestone in my research and scientific innovation journey.

    About the competition

    The International Chemistry Competition assesses and develops young people's knowledge of chemistry. Open to high school students and university students, it is held mainly online. Three categories: Junior for Grade 10 and below, Youth for Grades 11 to 13, and Senior for university students. The process includes three stages: Qualification Round with five problems worth five points each, free with a certificate; Semi Final Round with a supervised 60 minute test and three problems; Final Round with an online exam of 30 questions in 30 minutes. Topics include general chemistry, atomic structure, periodic table, chemical bonds, thermodynamics, organic and physical chemistry, and spectroscopy.

    My participation

    Participation in the IChC 2026 with qualification for the semi final stage. This experience allowed me to apply my chemistry knowledge in an international setting.

    About the competition

    The International Computer Science Competition assesses computer science knowledge, logic, and problem solving. Three categories: Junior, Youth, and Senior. The stages include Qualification Round with five problems and free access, Pre Final Round with a 60 minute test and three problems for a total of twenty points, and Final Round with a 40 minute online exam.
