01 — Passeport Carbone

Passeport Carbone

Digital tool for assessing greenhouse gas emissions of small and medium-sized enterprises.

Climate tech Environment Carbon assessment Digital tools

Research Context

Small and medium-sized enterprises play an important role in economic activity, but assessing their greenhouse gas emissions can be difficult when data collection and analysis tools are complex or not easily accessible.

Research Question

How can digital tools simplify the collection and assessment of data related to greenhouse gas emissions for small and medium-sized enterprises?

Approach

Passeport Carbone explores a simplified digital approach that allows companies to enter certain data related to their activities and energy consumption.

The prototype focuses notably on essential data such as electricity and fuel consumption, in order to contribute to building an assessment of the company's emissions.

Objective

The goal is to make the first steps of emissions assessment simpler, more accessible, and easier to understand for companies that do not necessarily have specialized tools.

02 — Bioenergy & Hydroponics

Bioenergy & Hydroponics

Integrated bioenergy and hydroponic agriculture system.

Renewable energy Sustainable agriculture Bioenergy Automation

Research Context

Organic waste represents both an environmental problem and a potential resource. At the same time, agriculture must face challenges related to the use of water, energy, and available resources.

Research Question

How can organic waste recovery, renewable energy, and hydroponic agriculture be combined within an integrated and sustainable system?

Research Concept

This work explores the combination of several technologies:

  • Biogas production from organic matter;
  • Use of photovoltaic energy;
  • Hydroponic cultivation;
  • Semi-automated vertical farming;
  • Monitoring of environmental parameters such as temperature, humidity, and pH.

General Principle

Organic Waste → Biodigester → Biogas → Energy

Solar Energy → Photovoltaic → System Power → Hydroponics

Objective

The goal is to explore how different sustainable technologies can be integrated into a single system in order to make the most of available resources and create a synergy between energy production and agricultural production.

04 — Methodology

Research Methodology

From problem to solution.

My approach consists in progressively transforming a question or a problem into a studied and documented solution.

01

Identify

Understand a real-world problem or a scientific question.

02

Research

Study existing knowledge, concepts, and the various possible approaches.

03

Design

Develop an idea, a model, or a solution.

04

Prototype

Turn the concept into a physical or digital prototype whenever possible.

05

06

Document

Present the approach, observations, results, and future perspectives.
