Gas Leak Prevention System

Engineering Safety Team Project Competition

Overview

A scientific and technological project developed as part of a team of five students, focused on preventing risks related to domestic gas leaks.

The goal is to research a solution that detects and limits the risks associated with gas leaks in indoor spaces, by combining several scientific and technological approaches.

The Problem

Domestic gas leaks can represent a significant risk in enclosed spaces. The project therefore seeks to explore an approach to improve prevention and safety through a technological system.

The Idea

Our team is working on a system incorporating a detection and prevention mechanism, with reflection on filtration/suction and the use of suitable materials, notably zeolite.

The system is also designed with a power supply based on a combination of solar energy + battery, in order to consider a certain degree of energy autonomy.

My Contribution

As a member and coordinator of the team, my contribution focuses on project coordination, communication, research, and presenting the idea.

Bioenergy & Hydroponics

Bioenergy Hydroponics Sustainability Engineering

Overview

A sustainable engineering project exploring the combination of bioenergy production and hydroponic agriculture.

The idea is based on recovering organic waste to produce biogas, combined with the use of photovoltaic energy to power certain elements of the system.

The Idea

The concept combines several fields:

Organic Waste → Biogas → Energy

Solar Energy → Agricultural System

The goal is to explore a model in which organic waste can be valued as an energy source, while a hydroponic system produces plants with optimized use of resources.

The project also envisions a form of semi-automated vertical farming, with various parameters that can be monitored and controlled.

Scientific Fields

Biology Chemistry Energy Agriculture Automation Engineering Environmental Sustainability

Vision

This project reflects my interest in technologies capable of simultaneously addressing issues related to energy, agriculture, and resource recovery.

Passeport Carbone

Digital Sustainability Prototype Research

Overview

Passeport Carbone is a digital prototype designed to simplify the assessment of greenhouse gas emissions for small and medium-sized enterprises.

The project stems from an observation: small businesses can face difficulties in measuring and documenting their emissions in a simple and accessible way.

The Problem

Assessing greenhouse gas emissions can require data and methods that some SMEs do not always have the means or tools to easily collect.

The Idea

Passeport Carbone seeks to simplify this process through a digital interface that allows a company to enter certain information related to its activities and energy consumption.

The prototype is notably designed around essential data such as:

  • Electricity consumption
  • Fuel consumption
  • Other relevant activity data

The information entered can then be used to produce an assessment report for the company.

Technologies

HTML CSS JavaScript Digital data collection Emissions assessment logic
