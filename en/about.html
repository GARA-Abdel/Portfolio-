Profile

I am Abdel Aziz Adama GARA, a young scientist and aspiring engineer from Burkina Faso. My journey is guided by a strong curiosity for science, engineering, and emerging technologies.

I am particularly interested in mathematics, physics, artificial intelligence, robotics, and the design of technological solutions. I enjoy understanding how systems work, analyzing problems, and finding ways to turn an idea into a concrete achievement.

Mathematics Physics Engineering Artificial Intelligence Robotics Innovation

02

Vision

I aim to build a path at the intersection of science, engineering, and technological innovation.

My focus is particularly oriented toward mechanical engineering, mechatronics, autonomous systems, and aerospace engineering. I want to progressively deepen my knowledge in these fields and develop the ability to design systems that address real-world problems.

I am also interested in the applications of artificial intelligence and robotics, as well as technologies capable of contributing to challenges such as energy, agriculture, and sustainability.

03

Academic Journey

My scientific path has been built progressively around a marked interest in scientific and technological disciplines. After my studies at Collège Charles Lwanga de Nouna, I joined the Lycée Scientifique National de Bobo-Dioulasso, one of the leading scientific institutions of excellence in Burkina Faso.

There, I am pursuing a demanding scientific education including mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, and computer science.

    Terminale C (12th grade, science track)

    Continuing my education at the Lycée Scientifique National de Bobo-Dioulasso, with the goal of obtaining the scientific baccalaureate and pursuing higher studies in the field of engineering.

  • 2026
